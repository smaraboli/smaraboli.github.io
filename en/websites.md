Here are some websites of interest:

[CubeCheck]

<a href="https://sites.google.com/redland.cl/cubecheck/home" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CubeCheck</a> 
Site made by me with written tutorials for solving the Rubik's Cube.

<a href="https://jperm.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">jperm.net</a> 
Made by J Perm, it is a great website for written and video tutorials for cubing as well as tools for practicing and algorithm memorization.

<a href="https://libraryofbabel.info/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Library of Babel</a> 
Made by Jonathan Basile and based on Jorge Luis Borges' short story "The Library of Babel", it is able to generate a library of all possible pages of 3,200 characters in books of 410 pages.
